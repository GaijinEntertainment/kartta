# Walk from a field, a variable or a constant to its users

- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Governs:** `crates/kartta-graph/src/calls.rs` (`dependents_of`, `user_edges`), `SegmentSymbol::users`,
  `Seed::user_count`, `Seed::consumers`
- **Pinned by:** `crates/kartta-graph/tests/graph/analyze.rs`
  (`a_constant_lists_the_symbols_that_read_or_write_it_and_its_blast_walks_on_from_them`,
  `a_seed_of_a_constant_lists_its_users_and_reaches_their_callers`, `a_function_has_no_users_however_its_name_is_read`,
  `a_seed_of_a_constant_counts_more_than_thirty_users_by_directory`)

A field's reads and writes are uses ([ADR 3.34](/design-docs/adr/3.34-field-and-value-uses.md)), not calls, so a blast
that walked callers alone reached nothing from a change to one. From a field, a variable or a constant the walk of
[ADR 3.22](/design-docs/adr/3.22-change-impact-analysis.md) takes its users and goes on through their callers; a
function's reach still follows its callers alone, at the price of building a data symbol's user edges at each expansion.

## Decision

- **Data symbols take users.** A walk that expands a field, a variable or a constant takes its users: the symbols whose
  calls or uses bind it, each once at its most confident site, in path order, never the symbol itself. Each symbol the
  walk reaches expands by its own kind: a field, a variable or a constant through its users, any other symbol through
  its callers.
- **Other kinds take callers.** A walk from any other kind takes its callers alone, however its name is read. A walk
  from a function that a table's initializer calls reaches the table, a variable, and goes on to the table's readers.
- **Segments.** A segment's data symbol lists its users beside its callers, under the same caps and floor as its
  callers. A changed data symbol counts its users in `user_count` and lists them, or their directories past 30, in place
  of its callers; `caller_count` still counts its callers.
- **Bands.** The bands of [ADR 3.22](/design-docs/adr/3.22-change-impact-analysis.md) keep their thresholds, and a user
  counts as a caller does, once a symbol.
- **Building user edges.** A data symbol's user edges are built at each expansion, where a caller's are borrowed from
  the query's memo; the uses they come from bind once a name for the query.

## Why

- Without users, `impact_of_edit` on `wire.Error.Code` of this repository's Go binding answered a reach of 0 beside the
  3 sites `find_references` lists, 2 reads and a write in 2 files.
- Of 143 sampled Go fields on the Go monorepo snapshot, 98 reach symbols at the MCP tools' bounds, 3 hops and 500
  symbols, where none did before (2026-10-23, #149).

## Consequences

- A segment of one data symbol pays the binding of its name's uses, which `find_references` pays for the same name: up
  to 118 ms at p99 for C++ on the game-engine tree (2026-10-23). Accepted.
- A reach through a variable can part from what `callers` lists: `cases.aztrUpper` of the snapshot's vendored
  `golang.org/x/text` reaches 6 symbols through `titleInfos` where `callers` lists 2. Accepted.

## Rejected

- **Uses for every kind, so a function taken as a value reaches whoever holds it** — a function's reach would part from
  `callers` wherever its name is read, and those reads are the guesses of
  [ADR 3.173](/design-docs/adr/3.173-data-use-binding.md)'s tiers.
- **A user counted once a site** — the bands would count lines where they count symbols.

## Revisit if

A sample of the users a data symbol's walk takes finds more than one in ten wrong, which would make its reach less
trustworthy than a function's. Not sampled yet.
