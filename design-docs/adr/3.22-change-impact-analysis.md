# Analyze what a change reaches with the walk the call queries take

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Governs:** `crates/kartta-graph/src/analyze.rs`, `crates/kartta-graph/src/change.rs`, `Graph::analyze_segment`,
  `Graph::analyze_change`, `Change::merge`, `ReachBand`, `SegmentQuery`, `ChangeQuery`, `Engine::analyze_change`
- **Pinned by:** `crates/kartta-graph/tests/graph/analyze.rs`, `crates/kartta/tests/engine.rs`
  (`a_change_says_why_no_graph_holds_each_region_it_counts_as_unheld`, `a_change_counts_its_regions_of_symlinks_apart`,
  `a_change_counts_its_regions_of_directories_apart`, `a_change_counts_its_regions_of_special_files_apart`,
  `the_seeds_of_several_families_compete_for_one_limit`)

An agent about to edit a range of lines asks what the edit reaches, and a reviewer asks the same of a whole change and
where its symbols' reaches meet. A blast is the walk `callers` takes, with a visitor in place of the list, under one
resolution memo for the whole query, so a walk that meets no field, variable or constant reaches what `callers`
lists, and a name's call sites resolve once; each query still pays that resolution, since calls resolve when a query
asks ([ADR 3.7](/design-docs/adr/3.7-call-resolution.md)).

## Decision

- **The walk.** The reach of a change to a symbol is the breadth-first walk over its callers that `callers` runs, under
  the same `Limits`: the depth bound, the symbol cap and the confidence floor. The walk gives each symbol it reaches to
  a visitor: `callers` and `callees` push a `Reached` into their list, and the analyses count the depth and the file of
  each symbol and read no definition for a symbol they do not list. A blast's reach is the length of the list
  `callers` returns for the same symbol and limits while the walk meets no field, variable or constant; from one of
  those the walk takes its users, which [ADR 3.132](/design-docs/adr/3.132-data-symbol-reach.md) decides. A blast
  counts the symbols the walk reached and never the root's call of itself, which the walk gives apart
  ([ADR 3.37](/design-docs/adr/3.37-call-walk-cut-counts.md)); a symbol's direct callers and callees read its edges,
  not a walk, and list that call.
- **One query resolves each name once.** A segment or a change keeps one resolution memo for every symbol it analyzes,
  so a name's call sites resolve once, whichever symbol or walk asks first. A walk borrows the memoized caller edges of
  each symbol it expands; the callees of a body are resolved for that body, as `callees` does. Nothing outlives the
  query.
- **A declaration answers for its definition.** A segment over a header lists a prototype with the callers and callees
  of the definition its callable identity links it to. A change counts a declaration and its definition as one changed
  symbol, answered as the occurrence the regions overlap first in path order.
- **A segment.** `analyze_segment` answers `None` for a file the graph does not hold, and lists the symbols whose lines
  overlap the range, `1..=u32::MAX` being the whole file. A range left open that way answers the last line a symbol of
  the file reaches as its end, never before its start, and an end the caller names below `u32::MAX` stays as asked. Each
  symbol carries its direct callers and callees, the count of its body's calls that bound nothing
  ([ADR 3.37](/design-docs/adr/3.37-call-walk-cut-counts.md)), and its blast, and the segment lists the files its file
  includes and is included by; the fields of [`Segment`](/crates/kartta-graph/src/analyze.rs) state their order. A
  segment is one file, which one language family holds, so the engine reaches it through `graph_of`.
- **A change.** `analyze_change` answers the symbols its regions overlap, each changed symbol with its walk, the
  footprint the walks reach together and where their reaches overlap, the hotspots two or more changed symbols reach,
  and the regions whose file the graph does not hold or that overlap no symbol; the fields of
  [`Change`](/crates/kartta-graph/src/change.rs) and `Hotspot` state each count.
- **Every count takes one floor.** The floor of `Limits` filters the direct callers and callees, the walk, and a
  changed symbol's caller count and consumers. `below_confidence` counts the direct calls under it, and for a walk the
  edges it turned down at each symbol it expanded.
- **The caller bounds every list.** `SegmentQuery` carries the walk's `Limits` and how many direct calls of each side a
  segment lists; `ChangeQuery` carries the `Limits` and how many consumers a changed symbol lists, and how many changed
  symbols and hotspots a change lists. A plain `Limits` converts to a query that lists everything, since the engine sets
  no bound its caller did not ask for ([ADR 3.25](/design-docs/adr/3.25-definition-source-text.md)). A bound cuts only
  its list, where the list is built: every walk and count still covers each changed symbol and each hotspot, and the
  bound caps the records held, the direct calls a segment resolves into definitions and the changed symbols a change
  keeps as **Ranking** says. The MCP server and the wire pass their own defaults
  ([ADR 4.23](/design-docs/adr/4.23-mcp-default-limits.md), [ADR 4.57](/design-docs/adr/4.57-wire-params-answers.md)).
- **A cut answer says how much it cut.**
  - Direct calls list at most the call limit of callers and of callees in path order, test and generated callers and
    users after the rest ([ADR 4.72](/design-docs/adr/4.72-caller-list-order.md)), each side with its `total` and
    `below_confidence`.
  - A blast and a changed symbol report `truncated` when the cap stopped the walk, `turned_away`, the callers or users
    the cap turned away at the symbol where it stopped, and `unexpanded`: the symbols at the depth bound, the symbol
    itself at a depth of 0, and those the cap left in the queue.
  - A change lists at most the seed limit of changed symbols and the hotspot limit of hotspots, and counts all of them
    in `changed_symbols` and `total_hotspots`; `truncated_seeds` counts the walks the cap stopped.
  - A changed symbol lists its direct callers while the consumer limit or fewer pass the floor, in path order with test
    and generated code last, and past that counts them by directory, the most first; a field, a variable or a
    constant lists its users in their place ([ADR 3.132](/design-docs/adr/3.132-data-symbol-reach.md)).
  - A segment's symbol and a changed symbol count in `ambiguous` the calls of their name that `Graph::callers` counts
    in `Walk::ambiguous`, which bound no definition because several of the name may be meant; the count ranks nothing.
    [ADR 4.12](/design-docs/adr/4.12-ambiguous-call-counts.md) states how the tools show it.
- **Ranking.** Changed symbols rank by reach, then by direct callers; hotspots by the changed symbols that reach them,
  then by the nearest depth; ties break by qualified name, file and line. Test, generated and entry code ranks after
  all other code in both lists. A change keeps at most twice the seed limit of listed changed symbols at a time, each
  with its definition and consumers, and cuts them back to the best within the limit; past the list it keeps only the
  position of each changed symbol and of each symbol the walks reach.
- **Affected files.** A blast's `affected_files` counts the files of its callers and of its file's includers, as
  [ADR 3.133](/design-docs/adr/3.133-blast-file-count.md) decides.
- **Bands.** A reach under 10 is `Low`, 10 to 49 `Medium`, 50 to 199 `High`, and 200 or more `Critical`. An exported
  symbol is `High` from 20 to 199, and an interface `Critical` from 50. No reach falls in two bands.
- **Across language families.** `Engine::analyze_change` sends each region of a held file to the family whose language
  claims it. A region whose path the index does not hold or holds as a symlink, a directory, a socket, a fifo or a
  device node, which no graph holds, or whose held file no language claims, counts as unheld, and the answer counts
  apart the regions of missing paths, those of symlinks, those of directories, those of such special files, those of
  unclaimed files, and those of files a language declines, per language and the reason it states
  ([ADR 3.1](/design-docs/adr/3.1-language-plugin-contract.md)), at one index lookup per region. Each family's graph
  analyzes its regions, and `Change::merge` sums the counts and ranks the changed symbols and hotspots of all families
  under one cut. The families hold different symbols, so no symbol counts twice in a sum.

## Why

- One walk answers both, so the reach of a walk that meets no data symbol cannot part from the callers `callers` lists:
  of the segment symbols whose name selects the same symbol for `callers`, 1,263 of 1,266 on the C++ game-engine tree
  reported the length of its list, every exception a name several definitions share on one minified line (2026-10-17,
  #56).
- A memo per query resolves each name once across the walks of one change, whose five regions reached 2,606 symbols on
  the C++ tree (#56), and keeps nothing after it, so the build pays nothing for the analyses.
- Test, generated and entry code ranks last because how it is run, not what it calls, sets its reach.
- Lists cut at fixed numbers left a consumer that wanted every direct caller, seed or hotspot no argument to ask for
  them, and the wire carried the same cut (#1244).
- An interface is `Critical` sooner because each of its implementors answers for its contract.

## Consequences

- Each symbol pays its name's resolution: a segment over a whole vendored header of 310 symbols with common names took
  0.3 s on the C++ tree, about 1 ms a symbol (2026-10-17). Accepted.
- A blast is as right as the call bindings behind it; the defects of the tiers of
  [ADR 3.7](/design-docs/adr/3.7-call-resolution.md) move reaches with them.

## Rejected

- **A walk of the analyses' own** — a second breadth-first walk to keep in step with `callers`, so the reach a blast
  reports and the callers `callers` lists could part.
- **Each walk returns its list, and the analyses count it** — a definition read and built for every symbol reached,
  where a changed symbol lists only its direct callers and a blast lists none.
- **A memo for each symbol analyzed** — each walk resolves again the names the walk before it resolved.
- **List caps fixed in the engine** — 50 direct calls, 30 consumers, 300 seeds and hotspots that no caller could raise
  (#1244).
- **The list bounds as fields of `Limits`** — `callers`, `callees` and `call_path` take `Limits` too, and would carry
  bounds they never read.
- **A segment on the engine** — a file belongs to one family, so the engine would only route the call that `graph_of`
  already routes.
- **An open end answered as `u32::MAX`, or as the 0 a wire caller sent** — one is a number nobody meant, and the other
  says less than the graph holds.
- **Every end cut at the last line a symbol reaches** — a hunk past the last symbol would answer other lines than it
  asked about, and code at file scope sits past that line.

## Revisit if

A consumer needs a whole large header analyzed faster than per-query resolution answers it, 0.3 s on the C++ tree, or
changes of thousands of symbols make the per-query memo outgrow the few megabytes a query holds. Neither is asked for.
