An agent reads a cut answer as a complete one unless the answer says what it cut. Each call walk of ADR 3.7 counts what
its cap, its depth bound and its confidence floor turned away, and gives back the root's call of itself, which it does
not list, as it meets them in the walk it already makes.

## Decision

- A walk over callers or callees reports the symbols the cap turned away at the symbol where it stopped, the symbols at
  the depth bound whose calls it did not follow, the symbols the confidence floor dropped, each counted once where its
  calls collapse to the most confident, and the other callables the selected name selects that it did not start from.
  ADR 3.35 lists the most ranked of those.
- The cap and the floor are tested after the visited check, so a walk that meets only symbols it already holds is not
  cut and counts nothing.
- A walk over callers or callees gives its root's call of itself as `Walk::self_call`: the confidence and line of the
  root's most confident call of itself, the first line of those at that confidence. The cap does not cut it. Where the
  confidence floor drops it, `below_confidence` counts it instead. The symbols reached never hold the root, so a symbol
  further out that calls itself is listed once, as every symbol is, and is no self-call; a blast reaches what the walk
  reached (ADR 3.22).
- A walk over callees also counts the calls of its bodies that bound nothing, so a body whose calls all reach code
  outside the graph does not read as a body that calls nothing.
- `references` reports the sites the floor dropped and the total before its limit; the limit bounds the answer, not the
  work.
- `call_path` reports what the cap turned away, the callers it reached at the depth bound, counted when reached so that
  a chain found early leaves none out, and the callers the floor dropped. A path from a symbol to itself is found with
  no hop.

## Why

An answer that leaves something out says so, and says how much wherever the engine counts it without extra work
(CLAUDE.md, `<answers>`). The walk already meets each symbol it turns away, so a count adds no resolution. A chain that
`call_path` does not find within its bounds is no proof that the code holds none.

The walk marks its root reached before it expands it, so without `self_call` the root's call of itself would be dropped
as a symbol already reached and counted nowhere, while `references` lists the call: a recursive function would read as
having only its other callers. The walk compares each of the root's own edges with the root once, and copies the one it
finds.

## Consequences

- A count says how many, not which: an agent that needs the cut symbols asks again with a higher cap, depth or floor.
- A self-call is as right as the binding behind it, and many are a namesake bound to the caller: on the C++ game-engine
  tree 2 of 30 sampled were true before C++ calls on fields bound through the field's class (2026-10-23). The rest are
  open resolution defects: Rust calls on a field (#248), Rust paths that end in the caller's name (#204), Rust calls on
  std and external types (#219), base and super calls (#186), and C++ overloads (#443).

## Rejected

- **The root listed as its own caller or callee** — counts the root among the symbols reached, which moves the answers'
  counts and their reach band, and turns a symbol that only calls itself from one with no callers into one with a
  caller.

## Revisit if

Counting a category takes a second pass over the walk or a resolution the answer does not need. High confidence: every
count is taken where the walk meets the symbol.
