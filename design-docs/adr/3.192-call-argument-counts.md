# Bind no definition a call's count of arguments rules out

- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Governs:** `crates/kartta-graph/src/resolve.rs` (`takes_the_arguments`, `takes_too_few_arguments`,
  `passes_too_few_as_near`, `overloads_can_lie_outside`), `crates/kartta-graph/src/scope.rs` (`resolve_by_proximity`),
  `crates/kartta-graph/src/stored.rs` (`StoredCall`, `StoredSymbol`), `crates/kartta-lang-daslang/src/walk.rs`
  (`parameters`, `piped_head`, `SELF_MACROS`, `BLOCK_QUERY`), `crates/kartta-lang-dshl/src/walk.rs` (`count_past_zero`),
  `kartta_lang::CallRef::arguments`, `kartta_lang::Symbol::most_arguments`, `kartta_lang::Symbol::fewest_arguments`
- **Pinned by:** `crates/kartta-graph/tests/graph/arguments.rs`, `crates/kartta-languages/tests/daslang_arguments.rs`,
  `crates/kartta-languages/tests/dshl_macro_arguments.rs`

Native code is outside the graph, so a daslang call of a native overload bound the script function of its name, even one
that takes fewer arguments than the call passes or requires more. A language may state how many arguments a call passes
and the most and the fewest a definition takes: a call binds no definition its count falls outside, and a name the count
narrowed binds by its nearness only where nearness alone singles a definition out. A stored call gives up the top 8 bits
of its line to hold the count, and a stored symbol the 7 bits beside its export mark.

## Decision

- **A count only turns away.** `CallRef::arguments` is what a call passes, `0` where the language counts nothing,
  `Symbol::most_arguments` the most a definition takes, `u8::MAX` for any number, and `Symbol::fewest_arguments` the
  fewest it requires, `0` for none. A definition whose most is below a call's count, or whose fewest is above a count
  past `0`, is no candidate of that call, in every tier that reads the candidates of a name. No count raises a candidate
  or a confidence, so a language that states none keeps its answers. A count stops at `u8::MAX` and a stored fewest at
  127, each still a bound, so a call past either binds as far as the bound shows.
- **A count of `0` shows no call short of a definition.** A use asks the tiers as a call of `0` arguments and a
  construction counts none, so `f()` binds a definition that requires more.
- **The object fills a method's implicit parameter.** A member call counts its object, and a method's counts leave its
  implicit parameter out, so a call with a receiver adds one to a `Method`'s most and fewest and nothing to a free
  function's. A namespace takes no object: a member call that counts one binds no namespace, and a bare call of a
  daslang module's name still binds the module, whose macro it runs.
- **A name the count narrowed binds no likely guess.** A definition the count turns away marks the name as a package
  gate does (`turned_away`), so the definition left does not bind as the name's one callable or as its one first-party
  definition. Of the one callable nearest the caller, of its directory or its parent directory:
  - where the call passes too few for a definition as near, nothing binds, since the count alone chose;
  - where the call passes too many for a definition anywhere, it binds at 15, below a `likely` floor;
  - where the call passes too few only for a definition farther off, it keeps the tier of its nearness.
- **A closed script narrows without a guess.** Under `IncludeReach::Script` no name comes from outside the repository,
  so a definition the count turns away marks nothing, and the rule above does not apply: the definition left binds at
  the tier that finds it.
- **Scope keeps its tier.** A call that fits one overload of its file, or of a file it requires, takes that overload at
  that tier's confidence.
- **What daslang counts.** A call counts its arguments, named ones among them, the value each pipe at its head or a
  trailing block gives it, and a member call's object. `f() <| g() <| b` passes `b` to `g`, as the compiler reads it. A
  construction counts nothing. A `def`'s most is each name of its parameter lists, those with defaults among them, and
  the `self` that `class_method` and `explicit_const_class_method` give a `def static`. Its fewest is each name of an
  entry without a default, less one for any other `def static`, which can write the `self` a member call's object
  fills.
- **What DSHL counts.** A macro call counts what its top-level commas split, as the shader compiler does, and a macro's
  most and fewest are both its parameters, since the compiler refuses any other count. Each runs one past the written
  count, so that a call of no arguments counts and turns away a macro of parameters, where `CallRef::arguments` `0`
  would count nothing. A call no macro of its name takes binds nothing.
- **A block query binds no `def`.** A daslang call of `query`, bare or through a module, that passes a block, written,
  piped or trailing, is a macro invocation under `MacroInvocations::Apart`, whatever its count. The count alone does not
  turn it away, since its block fills the one parameter of a `def` that takes a `Uri`. The call stays unresolved. A
  `query` that passes values binds by its count, and a member call `obj.query()`, which calls what its object holds,
  binds as any member call does.

## Why

- 5,089 of the 78,563 daslang pairs on the game-engine tree passed more arguments than their callee takes (2026-10-30,
  #473). The wrappers of `daslib/ast.das` read as calling themselves, and 3,620 of the pairs bound the ECS `query` to
  `uriparser_boost.query`, the one script function of that name.
- That rule removes 4,918 pairs, 30 of 30 sampled wrong, and adds 780. The 760 that scope or a module's name binds are
  about 89% right, of 232 sampled over four draws, 10 of the 12 false pairs of one draw replacing a pair already false.
- The 20 pairs it adds by nearness are 12 of 20 right, below the bar, so they bind at 15, beside 450 pairs of a
  narrowed name that bound at 30 or 50, 30 of 30 sampled wrong, as `empty(raidName)` bound `market_structures.empty`.
- 1,214 pairs of the game-engine tree still bound a block `query` to `uriparser_boost.query` after the count, 30 of 30
  sampled wrong (2026-10-31, #738). The entity system's native `query` takes such a block, or the `call_macro` of
  `daslib/decs_boost`.
- The fewest removes 4,779 of the 74,425 daslang pairs on the same tree, 30 of 30 sampled wrong (2026-10-31, #737), of
  which 2,387 bound `range(n)` to one pattern library's `range(pat, lo, hi)` and 705 `float3(1)` and its kin to
  the script generics of `daslib/builtin`. It adds 1,150, 55 of 60 sampled right over two draws, the 5 false ones each
  `fread(f)` of a file, which replaces a pair already false.
- A nearest definition the count alone chose binds wrongly: the 561 `push(x)` pairs that candidate added all bound
  `net_scope_adder.das` for the native `push`, of 617 such pairs.
- A definition passed too few shows less of a name than one that takes too few: binding at 15 on it demotes 107 pairs,
  19 of 27 sampled right, as `resolve_path(file)` and `log(msg)`.
- The DSHL count adds 11 pairs on the game-engine tree, all right, and removes 6, 4 of them false and 2 calls no macro
  of their name takes (2026-10-31, #827). Read through **A name the count narrowed binds no likely guess**, it demoted
  15 pairs to 15, all right, as `USE_HDR(ps)` on its own directory's macro, because a sample project's `USE_HDR()`
  narrowed the name.

## Consequences

- Overloads that differ by type alone stay beyond the count, at the tier of the scope that holds them, as
  `make_comment_reader` at `daslib/ast.das:651`, which passes what its wrapper takes and stays a self-call,
  `MenuItem(lab, shortcut, selected)`, a native overload that binds `imgui_boost.das:336`, which takes a block, and
  `fread(f)` of a file, which binds `fread(path)`: 2,469 pairs at tiers 70 and 80 name a function with a native binding
  of the call's count on the game-engine tree, 19 of 40 sampled false, so about 1,200 of those tiers' 56,354 pairs
  (2026-11-01, #739). Accepted: the graph holds no daslang types. The same shape at the tiers that bind by a name alone
  falls to [ADR 3.194](/design-docs/adr/3.194-daslang-call-scope.md).
- `f()` still binds a definition that requires more: about 19 pairs on the game-engine tree by a text search, as
  `quat4()` binds `quat4(x, y, z)`. Accepted.
- A DSHL call no macro takes loses its edge, though its name says which macro it means: 2 calls on the game-engine tree,
  each in a macro nothing calls, since a macro that expanded it would not compile. Accepted.
- A call past line 16,777,215 reads as that line. Accepted.
- Counting costs daslang's extraction 2.3% of its instructions on the game-engine tree, 112.9 G to 115.5 G (2026-10-31),
  and the heap at a build's peak holds no more, since every count fits in existing bytes. Accepted.
- A macro other than the two named that adds a parameter turns its callers away. Accepted: `daslib/class_boost` is the
  one such macro the removed sample shows.
- A script `def query` that takes a block binds no block `query`. Accepted: the game-engine tree and daScript's own
  tree hold no such `def`, and neither holds a `def find_query`, which the rule leaves out.

## Rejected

- **Check the same-file tier alone**, where #473 found the defect — 453 of the 5,089 pairs are same-file, so it leaves
  the other 4,636.
- **Mark a count of `0` as counted** — a mark on every stored call, for about 19 pairs.
- **Give the fewest a byte of its own** — takes a stored symbol from 28 to 32 bytes, 6.3 MB on the game-engine tree's
  1.57 M symbols, where 127 parameters are more than any definition requires.
- **Bind every call of a narrowed name at 15** — a shorter overload anywhere in the tree narrows a name, so it demotes
  4,001 pairs that bound the same before, 20 of 30 sampled right.
- **Bind at 15 every call the count changed** — hides 738 pairs under a `likely` floor, 30 of 30 sampled right on the
  draw that took the pool at 15, for the sake of the one in nine that is false.
- **Keep today's binding for a DSHL call no macro takes** — a rule in every tier that reads candidates, for 2 calls that
  never compile (#827).
- **Read a block parameter against a call's last argument** — separates `Begin` and `MenuItem`, about 30 of the false
  pairs, at a mark on every daslang symbol and call, and leaves `min(length(a), length(b))` bound to `linq.min`. Off
  `query` it removes 133 pairs, 16 of 30 sampled right, because a typedef another file declares, as `CopyCompCb`, reads
  as a type that takes no block.
- **Read an `[es]` block as the macro** — script functions take such blocks, so it removes 19 pairs that are right, as
  `find_flashlight_in_gun`, and it keeps 77 of the 1,214, whose blocks carry no `[es]`.

## Revisit if

A language takes up the count whose calls hide arguments its walk cannot count, daslang's macros add parameters beyond
`class_boost`'s or take them away, a project defines a script `query` that takes a block, or the shader compiler takes
a macro call of another count than the macro's parameters. Held with moderate confidence: the removed pairs were
sampled, not read whole.

## History

- 2026-10-31 — A closed script narrows without a guess: DSHL took up the count, and its names have no definitions
  outside the graph for a narrowed name to hide (#827)
- 2026-10-31 — A count only turns away: a definition that requires more than a call passes is turned away as well, since
  daslang's count holds what a call passes; the object and the nearest guess follow it (#737)
