# Bind no definition that takes fewer arguments than a call passes

- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Governs:** `crates/kartta-graph/src/resolve.rs` (`takes_the_arguments`, `takes_too_few_arguments`),
  `crates/kartta-graph/src/scope.rs` (`resolve_by_proximity`), `crates/kartta-graph/src/stored.rs` (`StoredCall`),
  `crates/kartta-lang-daslang/src/walk.rs` (`parameters`, `SELF_MACROS`, `BLOCK_QUERY`),
  `kartta_lang::CallRef::arguments`, `kartta_lang::Symbol::most_arguments`
- **Pinned by:** `crates/kartta-graph/tests/graph/arguments.rs`, `crates/kartta-languages/tests/daslang_arguments.rs`

Native code is outside the graph, so a daslang call of a native overload bound the script function of its name, even one
that takes fewer arguments than the call passes. A language may state how many arguments a call passes at least and how
many a definition takes at most: a call binds no definition that takes fewer, and a name the count narrowed binds by
its nearness only as a guess. A stored call gives up the top 8 bits of its line to hold the count.

## Decision

- **A count only turns away.** `CallRef::arguments` is a lower bound, `0` where the language counts nothing, and
  `Symbol::most_arguments` an upper bound, `u8::MAX` for any number. A definition whose upper bound is below a call's
  lower bound is no candidate of that call, in every tier that reads the candidates of a name. Neither count raises a
  candidate or a confidence, so a language that states neither keeps its answers. A count stops at `u8::MAX`, where it
  stays a lower bound, so a call past it binds any definition that takes `u8::MAX`.
- **The object fills a method's implicit parameter.** A member call counts its object, and a method's bound leaves its
  implicit parameter out, so a call with a receiver takes one more of a `Method` and none more of a free function. A
  namespace takes no object: a member call that counts one binds no namespace, and a bare call of a daslang module's
  name still binds the module, whose macro it runs.
- **A name the count narrowed binds no likely guess.** A definition the count turns away marks the name as a package
  gate does (`turned_away`), so the definition left does not bind as the name's one callable or as its one first-party
  definition. The one callable nearest the caller, of its directory or its parent directory, binds at 15, below a
  `likely` floor. Scope keeps its tier: a call that fits one overload of its file, or of a file it requires, takes that
  overload at that tier's confidence.
- **What daslang counts.** A call counts its arguments, named ones among them, the value a pipe or a trailing block
  gives it, and a member call's object. A construction counts nothing. A `def` counts each name of its parameter lists,
  those with defaults among them, and the `self` that `class_method` and `explicit_const_class_method` give a
  `def static`.
- **A block query binds no `def`.** A daslang call of `query`, bare or through a module, that passes a block, written,
  piped or trailing, is a macro invocation under `MacroInvocations::Apart`, whatever its count. The count alone does not
  turn it away, since its block fills the one parameter of a `def` that takes a `Uri`. The call stays unresolved. A
  `query` that passes values binds by its count, and a member call `obj.query()`, which calls what its object holds,
  binds as any member call does.

## Why

- 5,089 of the 78,563 daslang pairs on the game-engine tree passed more arguments than their callee takes (2026-10-30,
  #473). The wrappers of `daslib/ast.das` read as calling themselves, and 3,620 of the pairs bound the ECS `query` to
  `uriparser_boost.query`, the one script function of that name.
- The rule removes 4,918 pairs, 30 of 30 sampled wrong, and adds 780. The 760 that scope or a module's name binds are
  about 89% right, of 232 sampled over four draws, 10 of the 12 false pairs of one draw replacing a pair already false.
- The 20 pairs the rule adds by nearness are 12 of 20 right, below the bar, so they bind at 15, beside 450 pairs of a
  narrowed name that bound at 30 or 50, 30 of 30 sampled wrong, as `empty(raidName)` bound `market_structures.empty`.
- 1,214 pairs of the game-engine tree still bound a block `query` to `uriparser_boost.query` after the count, 30 of 30
  sampled wrong (2026-10-31, #738). The entity system's native `query` takes such a block, or the `call_macro` of
  `daslib/decs_boost`.

## Consequences

- Overloads that differ by type alone stay beyond the count, at the tier of the scope that holds them, as
  `make_comment_reader` at `daslib/ast.das:651`, which passes what its wrapper takes and stays a self-call, and
  `MenuItem(lab, shortcut, selected)`, a native overload that binds `imgui_boost.das:336`, which takes a block. About
  one added pair in nine is such a call. Accepted: the graph holds no daslang types.
- A call past line 16,777,215 reads as that line. Accepted.
- Counting costs daslang's extraction 2.3% of its instructions on the game-engine tree, 112.9 G to 115.5 G (2026-10-31),
  and the heap at a build's peak holds no more, since both counts fit in existing bytes. Accepted.
- A macro other than the two named that adds a parameter turns its callers away. Accepted: `daslib/class_boost` is the
  one such macro the removed sample shows.
- A script `def query` that takes a block binds no block `query`. Accepted: the game-engine tree and daScript's own
  tree hold no such `def`, and neither holds a `def find_query`, which the rule leaves out.

## Rejected

- **Check the same-file tier alone**, where #473 found the defect — 453 of the 5,089 pairs are same-file, so it leaves
  the other 4,636.
- **Count the parameters a call must fill as well** — a call's count is a lower bound, so it cannot show that a call
  passes too few.
- **Bind every call of a narrowed name at 15** — a shorter overload anywhere in the tree narrows a name, so it demotes
  4,001 pairs that bound the same before, 20 of 30 sampled right.
- **Bind at 15 every call the count changed** — hides 738 pairs under a `likely` floor, 30 of 30 sampled right on the
  draw that took the pool at 15, for the sake of the one in nine that is false.
- **Read a block parameter against a call's last argument** — separates `Begin` and `MenuItem`, about 30 of the false
  pairs, at a mark on every daslang symbol and call, and leaves `min(length(a), length(b))` bound to `linq.min`. Off
  `query` it removes 133 pairs, 16 of 30 sampled right, because a typedef another file declares, as `CopyCompCb`, reads
  as a type that takes no block.
- **Read an `[es]` block as the macro** — script functions take such blocks, so it removes 19 pairs that are right, as
  `find_flashlight_in_gun`, and it keeps 77 of the 1,214, whose blocks carry no `[es]`.

## Revisit if

A language takes up the count whose calls hide arguments its walk cannot count, daslang's macros add parameters beyond
`class_boost`'s, or a project defines a script `query` that takes a block. Held with moderate confidence: the removed
pairs were sampled, not read whole.
