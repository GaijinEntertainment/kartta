# Spell each count concept of an MCP answer with one word

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Governs:** `crates/kartta-mcp/src/render.rs`, `crates/kartta-mcp/src/render/`, `crates/kartta-mcp/src/server.rs`
  (the tool descriptions and the instructions), `crates/kartta-mcp/src/params.rs`
- **Pinned by:** `crates/kartta-mcp/tests/graph.rs`
  (`a_walk_the_node_cap_stops_counts_what_it_turned_away_and_cuts_no_list`,
  `find_call_path_writes_hops_for_a_chain_alone_and_zero_for_one_symbol`,
  `find_callers_lists_the_nearest_callers_up_to_its_limit_and_counts_every_one`,
  `find_callers_spends_its_limit_on_the_callers_its_role_keeps`,
  `find_references_lists_a_hot_field_by_file_most_sites_first_and_counts_what_it_leaves_out`,
  `find_unused_lists_what_nothing_reaches_and_counts_what_it_did_not_judge`), `crates/kartta-mcp/tests/stdio.rs`,
  `crates/kartta-mcp/tests/outline.rs`, `crates/kartta-mcp/tests/names.rs`

The MCP server's consumer is anyone's agent ([ADR 4.46](/design-docs/adr/4.46-release-scope.md)), which reads every
tool's answer with one reader. Every answer spells the four count concepts with one word each, gives a concept that
shared one of those words a word of its own, and the server's instructions state the four once. The trade is a break of
every answer's counts.

## Decision

- **The four words.** `total=` counts every record an element counted. `shown=` counts the records it lists, written
  only where it lists fewer than `total=`. `truncated="true"` marks an element whose list or body a limit or a fixed cap
  cut, beside the last line that names the way to the rest ([ADR 4.24](/design-docs/adr/4.24-mcp-omission-counts.md)).
  `hops=` is a distance along calls or imports: a ref's from the root, a hotspot's nearest, a chain's length, an
  importer's. Each word counts the element's own list: an envelope its matches, a match its callers, callees or sites, a
  list element its records.
- **A second kind of record.** An element that counts a second kind of record beside its own list names the count for
  the kind and its listed part `<kind>_shown=`: `files=` and `files_shown=`, `file_scope=` and `file_scope_shown=`.
  Its cut shows in `<kind>_shown=` and its closing line; `truncated=` marks the element's own list alone. A count of
  a kind the element does not list, as `calls=`, `users=` or a candidate's `callers=`, is no total.
- **A record that lists none.** A self-closed record with `total=` lists none of its records and carries neither
  `shown=` nor `truncated=`, as a `find_references` `<file>` past the 100 sites listed; its match's `truncated=`
  marks the cut.
- **Previews.** The `<candidate>` and `<suggestion>` records of the other symbols a name selects or nearly spells are a
  preview of at most five, counted in `passed_over=` or `one_edit=` and closed past five by the line that names the call
  listing them all ([ADR 4.24](/design-docs/adr/4.24-mcp-omission-counts.md)); they carry no `shown=` and set no
  `truncated=`.
- **Words of their own.**
  - A walk the node cap stopped carries `turned_away=`, the symbols the cap turned away, and no `truncated=`: the walk
    lists what it reached, and `total=` counts it. `impact_of_change` counts such walks in `stopped_walks=`.
  - A call path's bound is `max_hops=`, and the callers it reached at the bound `at_hops_bound=`. A match without a
    chain writes no `hops=`, so `hops="0"` is a path from a symbol to itself alone.
  - `depth=` is a `find_implementations` type's distance along its parents.
  - `excluded=` counts what a filter of the call left out: `grep`'s excludes, `list_dir`'s `skip_dotfiles`,
    `find_references`' `sites_under`. `find_unused` counts what it did not judge in `<unjudged/>`.
- **Reach.** `reach_band` grades an impact tool's reach over its fixed 3-hop walk by
  [ADR 3.22](/design-docs/adr/3.22-change-impact-analysis.md)'s bands, which each impact tool's description names with
  their thresholds. `find_callers` carries no grade: its `total=` is the count the grade is taken from, at the `hops`
  the call asks.
- **Where it is stated.** The `initialize` instructions state what the four words mean; each tool's description says
  what they count on its elements, and names the other attributes its answer carries. The C ABI wire keeps its own keys
  ([ADR 4.57](/design-docs/adr/4.57-wire-params-answers.md)).

## Why

- `count=` meant the records listed on `find_symbols`, `find_implementations` and `find_unused` and every record on
  `find_references`, `find_importers` and the impact include lists, and listed-of-total had six spellings, so an
  agent needed a reader per tool (#1037).
- `find_callers` graded its own count, so one symbol read `critical` at `hops` 3 and `high` at `hops` 1 (#1037).
- The engine sets a walk's `truncated` exactly when its cap turns a symbol away (`crates/kartta-graph/src/calls.rs`,
  `dependents.rs`), so the flag repeated `turned_away=`.
- Rewritten into these words, every base answer of the parity plans on dagor and the Go monorepo snapshot equals the
  new answer, 21,757 and 25,270 calls, and the answers are 0.17% and 0.20% smaller (2026-11-24).

## Consequences

- An agent written against the earlier words reads none of the counts. Accepted, before the release.
- A list a limit or a cap cut carries 17 more bytes of `truncated="true"`, and the instructions about 250 more bytes
  in every session. Accepted.

## Rejected

- **Fix only the words with two meanings, `matches`, `count` and `excluded`** — five spellings of listed-of-total
  stay, and a reader per tool with them.
- **`shown=` on every list** — it repeats `total=` on every list that was not cut.
- **`<kind>_total=` for a second kind's count** — it renames `files=` and `file_scope=`, whose kind names hold one
  meaning already.
- **`truncated` for a stopped walk too** — `turned_away=` already says it, and how many.
- **`find_callers` graded on the impact tools' 3-hop walk** — a second walk at any other `hops`, for a grade its
  `total=` already gives.

## Revisit if

An answer needs a count that none of the four words and no kind's name carries. High confidence: every count the
answers held fit one.
