# Guess no member near the caller for a C++ call on a value

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Updated:** 2026-10-17
- **Initiative:** `03-code-graph`
- **Governs:** `kartta_lang::CallsOnValues`, `crates/kartta-graph/src/scope.rs` (`resolve_by_proximity`),
  `crates/kartta-lang-cpp/src/lib.rs`
- **Pinned by:** `crates/kartta-graph/tests/graph/calls.rs`
  (`a_member_call_on_a_value_binds_no_nearby_member_where_the_language_guesses_none`)

A C++ member call on an operand whose class the walk does not see records the empty receiver (ADR 3.3), and what such a
value holds is most often declared far from the caller: a container, a smart pointer, a library type. Such a call binds
through its file, its includes and the one callable of its name, and takes nothing from the callables nearest the
caller.

## Decision

- Where `Conventions::calls_on_values` is `CallsOnValues::NoNearestGuess`, as C++'s is, a member call on the empty
  receiver takes nothing at the three proximity steps. It binds through the caller's file, its includes and their
  closure (ADR 3.42), and the one callable of its name.
- `CallsOnValues::NearestGuessed`, the default, keeps the proximity steps for such a call.
- A C++ call on a local or a parameter declared as a class binds through that class first (ADR 3.3), and a call on a
  field of the caller's class or of its bases through the field's class
  ([ADR 3.47](./3.47-bind-a-c-plus-plus-call-on-a-field-through-the-class-the-field-is-declared-as.md)); each comes to
  these tiers only where that lookup finds no member of the name.

## Why

Each way of choosing the member of a call on a value was sampled on calls of the C++ game-engine tree (2026-10-17):
nearness bound the member called in 15% of them, `targetMeshes.push_back()` a nearby class's `push_back` for an
`eastl::vector`, and the caller's own class in none.

## Consequences

- Right nearby callers go with the wrong ones: `dead_code` answered 2,702 more C++ definitions, and about half of those
  sampled had lost their one, nearby, caller, as `mScene->startWrite()` beside `NpScene` (2026-10-17).
- The sample predates ADR 3.3's declared-class receivers (2026-10-19); the calls this rule covers are now only those on
  an operand whose declaration the walk does not see.

## Rejected

- **Nearness only where the caller's includes reach the nearby file** — 35% of sampled calls right.
- **A member of the caller's own class, with or without the members it inherits, before or after the include tier** —
  at most 5% of sampled calls right; before the include tier it takes the member called from 45% of the calls.
- **The type the operand's name spells** — ADR 3.3 records why a name names no type.

## Revisit if

A new sample of calls on the empty receiver, taken after the declared-class receivers, shows nearness right far more
often than 15%. Medium confidence, since the sample predates that change.
