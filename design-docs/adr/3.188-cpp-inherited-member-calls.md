# Bind a C++ call a class does not declare through the class's bases

- **Date:** 2026-10-24
- **Governs:** `crates/kartta-graph/src/scope.rs` (`member_of_a_base`)
- **Pinned by:** `crates/kartta-graph/tests/graph/calls.rs`
  (`a_cpp_call_on_a_class_that_inherits_the_member_binds_what_a_bare_call_in_its_member_binds`)

C++ looks a member that a class does not declare up in its bases, and in a template over a dependent base it requires
`this->f()`. A call on a class the caller's file reaches
([ADR 3.180](/design-docs/adr/3.180-cpp-declared-class-calls.md)) that declares no member of the name binds the member
its bases declare, as a bare call in its member finds it, at the price of reading each such class's bases.

## Decision

- **Bases.** Where the file reaches the class but no member of the name in it, `this->f()` in a template over a
  dependent base or `d.f()` on a local of a derived class, the call binds the member of the class's bases that the
  lookup of a bare call in its member finds: a nearer base's member hides a farther one's, and members of two bases
  neither derives from leave the call ambiguous.
- **Namesakes of a base.** A class of a base's bare name whose body is not that base's, `xray::Unit` beside `Unit`,
  holds another class's member.
- **Guessed bases.** A base the graph locates only through a guess leaves the call to resolve as one on a value.

## Why

- C++ looks a member the class does not declare up in its bases, and in a template over a dependent base it requires
  `this->f()`. Through the bases, 16,374 more calls of the game-engine tree bind; all 90 sampled pairs added or moved
  name the right class, and none of 60 sampled pairs they replaced was right (2026-10-24, #175).

## Consequences

- Reading a class's bases holds 5.5 MiB more heap at the peak of a whole-graph resolution of the game-engine tree, and
  that resolution runs 11% faster, since fewer calls reach the tiers for a value (2026-10-24). Accepted.
- Of a base's overloads of the name, the call binds the first, whatever its arguments: 4 of 90 sampled pairs to a base
  were the right class's wrong overload or specialization (#443).

## Rejected

- **Search a class's bases in the order they are written, the first that declares the name, as a use of `this->f`
  does** — bound 7,108 fewer pairs: it reads no base inside a namespace.
- **Leave the call to a value's tiers where a namesake of a base lies outside that base's body** — bound 3,979 fewer
  pairs, the namesake most often a class of another project.

## Revisit if

A sample of the calls bound through a base finds the wrong class more than once in ten. High confidence: all 90
sampled pairs named the right class (2026-10-24).
