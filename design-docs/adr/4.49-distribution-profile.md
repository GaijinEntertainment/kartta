# Build the distributed artifacts with fat LTO and stripped symbols

- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Governs:** `Cargo.toml` (`[profile.dist]`, `[profile.release]`), `scripts/dist.sh`, `scripts/ffi-archive.sh`,
  `crates/kartta-ffi/src/lib.rs` (the `panic` check)
- **Implementation:** pending — no workflow builds the Node addon with the profile yet (#138)

A release downloads what the build leaves, and `release` keeps LTO off so that local builds stay quick. The artifacts a
release ships build with `dist`, a profile of their own that takes the smallest output measured, at about twice the
cold build, an index build that holds, and a backtrace without frames.

## Decision

- **Two profiles.** `release` serves local work. `dist` builds what a release ships: `kartta-mcp`, the static library
  and the Node addon.
- **Settings.** `dist` inherits `release` and sets `lto = "fat"`, `codegen-units = 1` and `strip = "symbols"`.
- **Panics.** `dist` states `panic = "unwind"`, because every C ABI entry point catches a panic
  ([ADR 4.2](/design-docs/adr/4.2-c-abi-wire.md)), and with `abort` a panic kills the host process. `kartta-ffi` refuses
  to compile under any other setting, so a consumer's profile cannot drop the promise either.
- **The archive.** `scripts/ffi-archive.sh` builds it through
  `cargo rustc --locked --profile dist -p kartta-ffi --crate-type staticlib`, then applies
  [ADR 4.58](/design-docs/adr/4.58-archive-symbols.md). Cargo passes no `-C lto` for a crate with both `staticlib` and
  `rlib`, so `cargo build` leaves the archive without LTO: 100 MB under these settings, where the command above gives
  38 MB.

## Why

- Against `release`, on macOS arm64 (2026-11-02), `kartta-mcp` falls from 35.5 MB to 29.7 MB, the archive from 66.1 MB
  to 38.3 MB and the Node addon from 31.8 MB to 28.0 MB.
- The index build holds: on dagor at 2 threads 1,621 G instructions against 1,624 G, and 1,093 MiB peak footprint
  against 1,094 MiB.
- `strip` takes 1.6 MB from `kartta-mcp` and 1.1 MB from the addon. It leaves the archive as it is and the addon's
  exports in place, and the addon loads in Node.

## Consequences

- A cold build of the three artifacts at 2 jobs takes 200 to 250 s, against 112 s under `release`. Accepted.
- A query session of 24,346 calls on the Go monorepo snapshot retires about 10% more instructions than under `release`,
  and takes 70.0 s against 68.5 s: 0.6 ms a `find_symbols` call. Accepted, as that decides nothing under 500 ms a call.
- A panic's backtrace on a shipped binary lists no frame; the panic's message and location remain. Accepted.

## Rejected

- **Thin LTO with 16 codegen units** — `kartta-mcp` at 35.7 MB and the archive at 51.5 MB, with no saving on the
  binary or the addon against `release`.
- **Thin LTO with one codegen unit** — larger than fat on all three artifacts, 32.4 MB for `kartta-mcp`, and slower on
  the same query session, at 73.3 s, for 35 s off a cold build.
- **`strip = "debuginfo"`, what `release` gets** — keeps a backtrace's frames, and leaves `kartta-mcp` at 31.2 MB.
- **`panic = "abort"`** — voids the promise `kartta.h` makes.

## Revisit if

A report needs function names from a shipped binary's backtrace, or a query's cost under `dist` shows in an agent's
wait. High confidence in the sizes on macOS; medium on Linux and Windows, where nothing was measured.
