# Bound each MCP tool's answer by a default limit

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Governs:** `crates/kartta-mcp/src/server.rs` (the limit constants), `crates/kartta-mcp/src/params.rs`
- **Pinned by:** `crates/kartta-mcp/tests/stdio.rs`, `crates/kartta-mcp/tests/graph.rs`

Each answer of [ADR 4.1](/design-docs/adr/4.1-mcp-server-tools.md)'s server lands whole in an agent's context. The
tools below stop at a default limit, the prompts' own for the tools they name, and the answer counts what the limit cut
([ADR 4.24](/design-docs/adr/4.24-mcp-omission-counts.md)), so a cut answer costs the agent a second call where it
needs more.

## Decision

- **`find_files` and `glob_files`.** These tools have no default limit: a `max_results` omitted or zero lists every
  match, and a list the cap cut ends with a line saying so ([ADR 4.10](/design-docs/adr/4.10-mcp-path-list-cuts.md)).
- **`grep`.** A search lists 100 matching lines, cuts a line to 1 KiB around its match with the bytes it lost, and
  skips files over 4 MiB; an unscoped search excludes `**/vendor`, `**/node_modules`, `**/dist` and `**/__pycache__`.
- **`list_dir`.** A listing shows one level and 1000 entries, and does not enter `vendor`, `node_modules`, `dist`,
  `build` or `__pycache__`.
- **`find_symbols`.** A search answers 20 hits; keyword matches that underfill the limit are topped up by fuzzy hits
  marked `fuzzy=true`.
- **`read_definitions`.** A read answers 3 definitions of each of at most 10 names, a body at most 500 lines, each line
  cut at 1,024 bytes; a definition ranks `same-directory`, `vendored` or `other` by directory, not by the imports of the
  asking file.
- **`find_callers` and `find_callees`.** `find_callers` walks 3 hops and `find_callees` 1 hop, and `find_callees` puts
  `at=` only on a direct callee, since the walk records the line of each call but not which callee further out made it;
  both stop at 500 symbols.
- **`find_call_path`, `find_implementations` and `find_importers`.** `find_call_path` walks 8 calls and 20,000
  symbols, `find_implementations` answers 500 implementors, and `find_importers` reaches 3 includes away.
- **`find_references`.** An answer counts every site, lists 100 and names 50 more files
  ([ADR 4.11](/design-docs/adr/4.11-mcp-reference-listing.md)).
- **`impact_of_edit` and `impact_of_change`.** Both run [ADR 3.22](/design-docs/adr/3.22-change-impact-analysis.md)'s
  walk at 3 hops and 500 symbols. `impact_of_edit` lists 50 direct callers and callees; `impact_of_change` lists 20
  changed symbols and 20 hotspots, and 10 direct callers of each, or their directories past 30 callers. A change
  arrives as regions or a diff ([ADR 4.20](/design-docs/adr/4.20-change-diff-input.md)).
- **`find_unused`.** The tool judges every kind by [ADR 3.23](/design-docs/adr/3.23-dead-code-judgment.md), under an
  optional `path_prefix`, and lists 50 in path order across the graphs; an answer that judged every definition and
  found each reached says so.
- **`index_repo` and `index_stats`.** They count each graph's `calls`, `uses` and `includes`, not resolved edges, which
  [ADR 3.7](/design-docs/adr/3.7-call-resolution.md) does not build. With `coverage`, `index_stats` resolves every
  call ([ADR 3.24](/design-docs/adr/3.24-graph-stats-and-coverage.md)) each time it is asked, and adds a
  `<coverage/>` record for each language that holds a file.

## Why

- The tools the prompts name keep the prompts' defaults, so an answer has the size those prompts were written for
  (#74).
- The walk records the line of each call but not which callee further out made it, so a callee past the first hop
  answers no line.

## Consequences

- An agent that needs a callee past the first hop, or more than a limit lists, makes a second call. Accepted.

## Rejected

- **`find_callees` 3 hops deep by default, as `find_callers`** — a callee past the first hop carries no line of its
  call, and the question asked is what a body calls.
- **Coverage on every `index_stats`, or cached per build** — every first call would pay 0.1 to 0.4 s on the measured
  trees and a resolution's memory for counts most calls do not read, and a cache saves only a repeat.

## Revisit if

The prompts in use change the defaults they pass, or agents raise a tool's limit on most of its calls. Medium
confidence; how often agents raise a limit is not measured.

## History

- 2026-10-18 — The limits of `find_callees`, `impact_of_change`, `find_unused` and `index_stats` `coverage`: no tool
  answered what a symbol calls, what a change reaches, which definitions nothing reaches, or how well the graph's calls
  bind (#77)
