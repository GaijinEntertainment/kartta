# List a symbol's call of itself apart from its callers and callees

- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Governs:** `crates/kartta-mcp/src/render/calls.rs` (`self_call`, `caller_match`, `callee_match`, `no_callers`),
  `Walk::self_call`, the `find_callers` and `find_callees` tools
- **Pinned by:** `crates/kartta-mcp/tests/graph.rs`
  (`find_callers_find_callees_and_find_references_agree_on_a_function_that_calls_itself`,
  `a_function_that_calls_itself_alone_has_no_caller_and_no_callee_but_itself`)

A walk marks its root reached before it expands it, so the walk drops the root's call of itself as a symbol already
reached and counts it nowhere, while `find_references` lists the call. The callers and callees answers list that call
as one record of its own, which the counts and the reach leave out.

## Decision

- **The record.** A `find_callers` or `find_callees` match lists the root's call of itself as one `<self_call at=line/>`
  record ahead of its refs, its line in the match's file ([ADR 4.70](/design-docs/adr/4.70-mcp-answer-locations.md)),
  with the confidence mark a call carries, whatever `role` keeps. The public layer carries it as `Walk::self_call`
  ([ADR 3.37](/design-docs/adr/3.37-call-walk-cut-counts.md)), and the C ABI as `self_call`
  ([ADR 4.2](/design-docs/adr/4.2-c-abi-wire.md)).
- **The counts.** `count=` and the reach band leave it out. Under a floor that drops it, `below_confidence=` counts it.
  A symbol the walk reaches further out that calls itself is listed once, as every symbol is.
- **The notes.** A match whose only call is its own says so: "no callers but itself - entry point, dead code, or
  dynamic dispatch", "no call but its own binds it - ...", "no callees but itself - no other call in its body binds
  ...".
- **The other tools.** The other tools set no call of itself apart. `find_call_path` never passes a symbol twice, and a
  symbol reaches itself with no hop. The direct callers and callees of an `impact_of_edit` symbol and an
  `impact_of_change` seed list its call of itself, since they read its edges rather than a walk, and its reach counts
  the symbols other than itself.

## Why

- A recursive function answered its callers without the recursion, and a body whose one call binds itself answered
  "no callees", while `find_references` listed the call: the tools disagreed on the graph's own edge.
- A false self-edge was as invisible: `Php::extract` answered "no callers" beside a reference at its own line (#205).
- Recursion keeps nothing alive ([ADR 3.23](/design-docs/adr/3.23-dead-code-judgment.md)), so a self-call is not a
  caller: counted as one, it would answer a caller where a symbol is reached by nothing else.

## Consequences

- The record is a precision cost where the graph's own edge is false: of 30 C++ self-calls sampled on the C++
  game-engine tree, 2 are recursion (2026-10-23). The false kinds are tracked: a call on a value inside the namesake
  member it delegates to (#248), and another overload of the name (#443). Every false one was already in
  `find_references` and the impact tools' direct callers.

## Rejected

- **The root listed as its own caller and callee in the refs** — on the Go monorepo snapshot it moved `count=` in 151
  of the 157 changed answers and took the note from the 43 whose symbol calls only itself, answering a caller for 6
  that name an entry point, dead code or dynamic dispatch (2026-10-23).

## Revisit if

The graph binds a call on a value to the type the value holds, so few self-edges are false. The false kinds above are
open issues.
